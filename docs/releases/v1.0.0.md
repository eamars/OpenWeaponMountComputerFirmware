# Firmware v1.0.0

The first release since v0.0.1 (2025-09-26). How each part was decided and checked on the unit is in its ADR under [docs/developments_plans](../developments_plans/README.md).

## What is new

**Level view**
- A band along the bottom: the countdown timer's bar and time, and the current dope target (range, elevation, wind and gust, each with its own side). Tap the band for the next target, hold for the previous; both go round the card. "Next target on recoil" steps on a detected shot and stops at the last target.
- Faster, steadier drawing: partial redraw of the level, tearing sync, staging buffers.

**Dope card** (ADR-007)
- Targets entered by hand on the dope tile, with the shared value editor; a card of 10, 12 (default) or 15 targets; names 1, 2, 3 or A, B, C.
- The Kestrel link: the dope tile pulls the targets from a Kestrel, paired in HUD (device) mode or picked from the Kestrels nearby in phone mode. A pull fills the first 10 targets.

**Timer** (ADR-006)
- The timer tile rebuilt for the thumb; an editor step of 5, 10 or 15 s; start on the first shot or the button.

**Settings** (ADR-008)
- Dark pages with large full-width rows that stop on whole rows, a header with Back, Wi-Fi and battery on every page, and the panel's corner rule.
- A change is stored when it is made; Reset and Forget network ask first. Rows that do nothing in the unit's state are not shown.
- The power button's menu (hold the button): Reboot, Power off, Cancel.

**Updates over Wi-Fi**
- The offer shows the version and the release note; the update screen shows its steps and progress, then Restart.
- A 2.4 MB update takes about 22 s on a local network.

**Under the hood** (ADR-002, ADR-003, ADR-005)
- ESP-IDF 6.1; memory moved to PSRAM; a crash report and core dump kept across a restart.
- A review of every module and its bug fixes: Wi-Fi reconnects after a lost connection, a touch wakes the unit without acting, the BNO085 is reset properly at start, the PMIC's charge settings apply when saved, failures are shown instead of restarting.

## Updating a unit

Over Wi-Fi, from the repo on a computer on the unit's network:

```bash
python scripts/ota_host.py --note-file docs/releases/v1.0.0_note.txt
```

Restart the unit: after its Wi-Fi connects it finds the update, and Settings shows "Update available" at the top. Units already on v1.0.0 are not offered it. Over USB: `idf.py -p <port> flash`.

## Known limits

- Updates come over plain HTTP from a host on the local network and are not signed; the image's checksum rejects a damaged download. Run the host only on a network you trust.
- The update check compares major, minor and patch only: builds after v1.0.0 that are not tagged read as 1.0.0 and are not offered to a 1.0.0 unit (`--force` on the host offers them).
- The second update source, a central server, is a placeholder and not enabled.
